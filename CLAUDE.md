# Jessica portfolio

## Learned Patterns

- Use `overflow-x: clip` on the page wrapper; `overflow-x: hidden` creates a scroll container that prevents the sticky navigation from following the page scroll.
- Portfolio styling uses `tokens.css`; keep factual geology credentials in the CV content while excluding geological visuals and scroll decorations from the clinical AI design.
- The portfolio’s preferred accent is teal, with matching tinted surfaces and SVG identity assets; preserve the clinical AI layout when changing its palette.
